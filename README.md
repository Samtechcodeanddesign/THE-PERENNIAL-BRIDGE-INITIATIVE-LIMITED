# The Perennial Bridge Initiative Limited

## Building a Generation of Global Leaders

The Perennial Bridge Initiative Limited is a modern institutional website designed for an interfaith theological and ordination institute focused on spiritual formation, theological education, leadership development, and global ministry.

The website presents the organisation as a prestigious, accessible, and internationally minded institution while providing visitors with information about its programmes, mission, leadership pathways, and opportunities to make enquiries.

---

## Project Overview

The Perennial Bridge Initiative website was built with a strong emphasis on:

- Institutional credibility
- Spiritual and theological education
- Global leadership development
- Modern academic presentation
- Responsive design
- Smooth animations
- Clear navigation
- Direct visitor engagement
- WhatsApp communication

The visual identity uses an **Oxblood and White** colour system to create a distinctive, authoritative, elegant, and professional appearance.

---

## Main Features

### 1. Premium Hero Section

The homepage opens with a large institutional hero section featuring:

- Organisation branding
- Main mission statement
- Supporting description
- Call-to-action buttons
- Institutional statistics
- Scroll indicator
- Animated background

Main headline:

> **Building a Generation of Global Leaders**

Supporting message:

> **Awaken the Mature Gospel. Transform Lives. Forge Your Ministry.**

---

### 2. About Section

Introduces visitors to The Perennial Bridge Initiative and communicates its purpose, philosophy, and institutional vision.

---

### 3. Global Leadership

Explains the organisation's approach to developing leaders who can serve across cultures, communities, churches, and institutions.

---

### 4. The Call

Communicates the spiritual and professional significance of responding to a ministry and leadership calling.

---

### 5. Programmes

The website presents four core programme pathways:

1. **Spiritual Awakening**
2. **Sacred Text & Wisdom**
3. **Inner Transformation**
4. **Global Leadership & Service**

Each programme is presented using interactive cards with hover animations.

---

### 6. Flagship Programme

A dedicated section highlights the organisation's flagship educational or formation programme.

---

### 7. The Journey

An interactive timeline presents the visitor's progression through the institute's formation and ordination journey.

---

### 8. The Mature Gospel

A dedicated section communicating the organisation's emphasis on spiritual maturity, deeper Gospel understanding, transformation, and responsible leadership.

---

### 9. Who We Serve

The website identifies key audiences including:

- Ministers & Leaders
- Spiritual Seekers
- Global Bridge-Builders
- Lifelong Learners

---

### 10. Information Request

Visitors can submit their email through the information form to express interest or request further information.

The current implementation provides front-end form handling and local browser storage.

> **Note:** An external email service or backend has not yet been connected.

---

### 11. WhatsApp Integration

A floating WhatsApp button allows visitors to contact the organisation directly through WhatsApp.

The WhatsApp number should be configured in `index.html`.

---

### 12. Social Sharing

The website includes a share feature using the browser's Web Share API where supported, with a clipboard fallback for unsupported browsers.

---

### 13. Responsive Design

The website is designed to work across:

- Desktop computers
- Laptops
- Tablets
- Mobile phones

The navigation, cards, typography, forms, and layouts automatically adapt to smaller screens.

---

### 14. Animations

The website includes:

- Scroll reveal animations
- Hero entrance animations
- Animated hero background
- Button hover effects
- Card hover effects
- Programme card animations
- Timeline interactions
- Rotating symbolic rings
- Floating ambient effects
- WhatsApp pulse animation
- Back-to-top animation
- Social icon hover effects

The site also includes support for users who prefer reduced motion.

---

The project is built using standard frontend technologies:

- Font Awesome
